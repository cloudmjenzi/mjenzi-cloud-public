Introducing the Mjenzi Cloud Platform

In-country cloud for Africa and Australia. Use ours and pay monthly, or have us build the same cloud on hardware you own.

Mjenzi Cloud team · 6 November 2026 · 3 min read

Most businesses in Africa and Australia don't need another hyperscaler. They need a cloud that is close to their users, keeps data in-country, and is backed by people they can actually call.

That is what we have built. Today we are bringing it together as the Mjenzi Cloud Platform: one set of products, one network and one portal across five regions.

What's on the platform

  • Virtual compute: KVM virtual machines on all-NVMe storage, with block storage and snapshots.
  • GPU cloud: dedicated NVIDIA GPUs for training and running AI models, as part of Mjenzi AI.
  • Bare metal: single-tenant servers on the same private network as your VMs.
  • Object storage: S3-compatible, so your existing tools and backup software just work.
  • Backup-as-a-Service: Veeam-powered backup for servers, databases and Microsoft 365, with disaster recovery between regions.
  • Hybrid and Layer 2 connectivity: extend your on-premises network into the cloud as if it were one data centre.
  • Managed Kubernetes and security from our Enterprise SOC.

Everything runs on open technology: OpenStack, KVM, Ceph and Kubernetes. Your images, data and automation move in and out freely.

Two ways to get it

Mjenzi is Swahili for "builder", and that's what we do: we build cloud, AI and server infrastructure. You choose whether to rent ours or own your own.

1. Use ours (a service)

Run your workloads in our regions and pay monthly, with local support and in-country hosting. Start with the Cloud Configuration Sheet to put together a setup we can price.

2. Build yours (hardware you own)

We supply the servers, storage and switches, then build the same open cloud in your data centre. You own the hardware, and we can run it for you if you like. See private cloud.

Either way, our FDE engineers can help you plan, migrate and hand over. And if you're an MSP, telco or IT reseller, you can sell our cloud under your own brand.

Why now

Demand for AI and for sovereign hosting is rising across both continents at the same time. Data protection laws in Kenya, South Africa, Botswana and Australia are maturing, and GPU capacity is still scarce close to home. Local providers, from MSPs to telcos, are best placed to serve this demand, but few want to build and operate a cloud from scratch.

The Mjenzi Cloud Platform lets them launch in weeks instead of years.

Not sure whether to rent or own? Talk to an engineer and we'll compare both for you.
