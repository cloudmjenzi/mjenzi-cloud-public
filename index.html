Mjenzi AI

Sovereign AI, built where your data lives.

Most organisations in Africa and Australia send their data offshore to use AI. Mjenzi AI gives you GPU capacity and a private AI platform in-region, so sensitive data never has to leave the country.

  • GPU cloud on demand or reserved: NVIDIA and AMD Instinct accelerators for training and inference.
  • Private AI platform with GPU orchestration (Run:ai, Kubernetes), model serving and vector databases.
  • Open and commercial models hosted in-region behind your own network boundary.
  • On-prem AI clusters designed, built and supported by us when you need your own hardware.
