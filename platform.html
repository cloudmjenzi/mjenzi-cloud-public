Products

One platform, one network and one portal. Every product shares the same private networking, so VMs, GPUs, bare metal and storage work together as one environment.

GPU Cloud

Dedicated NVIDIA and AMD GPUs for training, inference, rendering and VDI.

Bare Metal

Object Storage

White-label & Wholesale

Launch your own cloud without building one.

MSPs, telcos and system integrators can sell Mjenzi Cloud as their own product. We run the infrastructure; you own the customer relationship, the brand and the margin.

  • Your pricing. Set your own price list for each reseller and customer, with usage exports for your billing system.

  • Partner-first. We back your sales team with pre-sales engineering, training and co-marketing.

Mjenzi EdgePoP

Our cloud, in your data centre.

For data centre operators, telcos and large enterprises that want a cloud inside their own facility. We supply, install and operate the full Mjenzi Cloud stack on site, connected to your networks and branded as yours.

  • Data never leaves your building, which helps with the strictest sovereignty and regulatory requirements.
  • Uses your networks and upstream providers, with simple Layer 2 controls.
